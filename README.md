# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — The World in One Picture

A newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026 world news in
one image: 43 stories from 6 continents, from the Crimean Bridge strike and
the Iran–US clash in the Gulf to the first Quad summit in Manila. A huge
serif masthead, three large illustrated lead stories, ten smaller illustrated
stories, a world map with numbered pins, a numbered brief list, and a slim
"By the Numbers" strip under the masthead. Every story happened or was
first reported on that Tuesday; contested claims are marked as claims.

The image is drawn deterministically by `draw.py` (Python + Pillow, no
network, no randomness) and copied to the output directory by `main.py`.
Run `python3 draw.py` to regenerate `entry.png` (1024x1024, byte-identical
on every run).
